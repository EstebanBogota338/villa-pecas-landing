FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT EVERY GROUP ASKS FIRST.

Anything else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we will get back to you.

What is the maximum capacity at Villa Pecas? +

The villa sleeps 22 guests. We have 7 bedrooms: six of them are triples and one is a quad, all with private baths.

Where exactly is it, and how do I get there? +

We are on the border between Restrepo and Cumaral, a strategic spot to reach both towns quickly. We send you the exact location on WhatsApp once your booking is confirmed.

Is the price per person or for the whole villa? +

COP $1,320,000 per night is for the whole villa (up to 22 guests). In low season we have special rates — just ask us on WhatsApp and we will work something out for your group!

What is included in the stay? +

The villa comes with fresh sheets for everyone, a full set of kitchen utensils, a fridge and a BBQ area ready to use.

Is there hot water in the bathrooms and kitchen? +

We are in a warm-climate area, so there is no hot water — but we promise the Meta weather will make you forget all about it.

Can we host parties or events? +

Yes! In fact, we have hosted unforgettable celebrations, including quinceañera parties.

Is there cleaning service during the stay? +

Yes, cleaning is included in the price. One less thing for your group to worry about.
